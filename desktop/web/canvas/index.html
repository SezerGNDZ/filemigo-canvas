Shortcuts & tips

Click or drag a shape to add it. Drag on empty space to select an area. Shift adds to your selection; Alt selects inside a group. Drag a pink connection dot onto another object, or drop in empty space to choose and connect a new shape. Space + drag pans, Ctrl/⌘ + wheel zooms. Shift keeps proportions when resizing.
